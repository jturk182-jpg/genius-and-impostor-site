The Intelligence Line

Domain-Specific Intelligence

Intelligence is not one general thing you have more or less of. It is the ability to solve particular problems in particular places, built entirely by training in those places. A bat beats you at echolocation and you beat the bat at language, and neither of you is smarter in general, because there is no such thing as being smart in general.
