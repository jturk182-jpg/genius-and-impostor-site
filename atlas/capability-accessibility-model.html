The Access Line

The Capability-Accessibility Model

What you can do and what you can do right this second are two different things. Everything you’ve built is in there, but at any given moment you can only get to some of it, and which part depends on the state your brain is in. That gap is why you can know something cold and still come up empty when it counts.
