The Bias Line

Trained Reasoning

Reasoning feels like the most general power the mind has, a logic engine you can point at anything. The brain has no such engine. What we call reasoning is trained pattern recognition, built like any other skill, which is why the exact same logic problem is easy in a familiar setting and nearly impossible in an abstract one. And because it is built, it can be taught.
