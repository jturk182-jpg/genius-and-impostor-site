The Genius Line

Adult Learning

Your brain keeps its ability to change for your whole life. Adult brains change less mainly because less needs changing, and the more you keep learning, the easier learning stays.

On the map
run byThe Genius+
Adult learning is done by the same learning engine that built your skills as a child.
requiresThe Goldilocks Zone+
Adults learn the same way children do, through practice at the right level of difficulty.
opposesThe Special Brain Story+
The belief that it’s too late to learn is a version of the Special Brain Story, and it prevents the learning that would prove it wrong.
The book devotes a chapter to the evidence that the adult brain keeps its ability to change, and to the seven reasons it seems otherwise.
