The Genius Line

Genius-Led Learning

There are two ways to learn and they are nowhere near equally good. One runs on curiosity, builds as it goes and lives on fast feedback, which is how the Genius works. The other hands you information to receive and tests you on it months later. You can tell which one you got by looking at what you still have.
