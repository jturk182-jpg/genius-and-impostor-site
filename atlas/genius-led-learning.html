The Genius Line

Genius-Led Learning

There are two ways to learn and they are not equally good. One runs on curiosity, builds as it goes, and lives on feedback, which is how the Genius works. The other runs on grades and approval, takes facts in passively, and tests only to score. School mostly runs the second one.

Impostor-led learning chases grades and approval, which is the Impostor running the classroom.
The book draws the line between the two modes of learning and shows how schooling trades the good one for the other.
