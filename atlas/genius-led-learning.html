The Genius Line

Genius-Led Learning

There are two ways to learn, and they are nowhere near equally effective. Genius-led learning is driven by curiosity, built through your own exploration, and guided by ongoing feedback. Impostor-led learning is driven by grades and status, and relies on receiving information and being tested on it later. The first builds knowledge and skill that lasts. The second mostly produces knowledge that is shallow and quickly forgotten.
