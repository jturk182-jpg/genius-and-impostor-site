The Bias Line

Automaticity Blindness

Once a skill runs on its own, you cannot feel it working anymore. You can only notice the effortful parts of your own mind, so the automatic parts go silent and start to look like nothing at all, even when they are the most complex thing you do.

On the map
causesEffort Inversion+
Because we cannot feel automatic skill, we misread its ease as simplicity. That misreading is Effort Inversion.
hidesEveryday Genius+
This blindness is exactly why we fail to see the everyday feats as the genius they are.
part ofIntrospective Bias+
It is one specific case of the larger trap of modeling the mind only from what we can feel.
The book treats this blindness as the root of a whole family of errors about intelligence. This is the mechanism underneath the biases.
