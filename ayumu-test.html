The Ayumu Test

Ayumu is a chimpanzee. He lives at a research institute in Japan, he is very good at puzzles on touchscreens, including the one he’s about to beat you at.

The game is simple. Numbers flash up on the screen and then vanish. You tap the spots where they were, in order from smallest to largest. That’s it.

In the version you are about to play, five numbers flash up for 210 milliseconds. That’s a literal blink of an eye. Ayumu gets it right about four times out of five.

You will start with three numbers and all the time you need, just to learn how the game works. Then you will try it Ayumu’s way.

  • Time needed Two minutes
  • Warm-up 3, then 4, then 5 numbers
  • Then try to beat Ayumu
  • Your tapping Never timed
  • Your results Stay on this device
