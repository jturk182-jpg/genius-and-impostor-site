The Ayumu Test

Ayumu is a chimpanzee. He lives at a research institute in Japan, he is very good at puzzles on touchscreens, and he is about to beat you at one.

The game is simple. Numbers flash up on the screen. They vanish. You tap the spots where they were, smallest number first. That is the whole thing.

Now the catch. Ayumu plays with nine numbers, and they are on the screen for 210 milliseconds. That is about a fifth of a second, roughly one blink. He gets it right about four times out of five.

You will start with three numbers and all the time in the world, just to learn the rules. Then you will try it Ayumu's way. Take a breath first.

  • Time needed Two minutes
  • Warm-up 3, then 4, then 5 numbers
  • Then Ayumu's nine
  • Your tapping Never timed
  • Your results Stay on this device
